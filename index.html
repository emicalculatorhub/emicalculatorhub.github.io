Frequently asked questions

What is an EMI?

An equated monthly instalment is the fixed amount you pay each month to repay a loan, covering both interest and principal.

How is EMI calculated?

From the loan amount, the monthly interest rate and the number of months, using the standard reducing-balance formula.

What is the difference between flat and reducing rates?

A flat rate charges interest on the full loan for the whole tenure, while a reducing rate charges it only on the balance that remains.

Does a longer tenure always help?

It lowers the monthly EMI but raises the total interest, often by a large amount.

Can prepayment save money?

Yes. Paying a lump sum early cuts the interest on every remaining month, and the saving is usually large.

How much loan can I afford?

Lenders often use a guideline of keeping total EMIs below about half of take-home pay, though rules vary by lender and country.

Is the calculator free?

Yes, every tool on this site is free and runs in your browser with no sign-up.

Do you store my loan details?

No. All calculations happen in your browser and nothing is sent or stored.
